## Inside our Los Angeles production floor

<img src="/images/shop/production-floor.webp" width="750" height="1000" loading="lazy" alt="TBSP production floor in Los Angeles with screen printing presses, folding tables and boxed blank shirts" style="max-width:420px;width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px" />

Blanks come in boxed by the case, get checked against the order, and move to the press, the embroidery machines or the printers. Finished pieces are folded, counted and packed on the same floor before they ship or wait for pickup.

## What's on the floor

- **Automatic screen printing presses** for production runs, from a dozen shirts to thousands. See [screen printing](/screen-printing/).
- **Manual screen printing presses**, including a Vastex V-2000HD, for samples, short runs, oversize prints and specialty inks like puff and foil.
- **Multi-head embroidery machines** for caps, polos, jackets and workwear, including 3D puff embroidery. See [embroidery](/services/embroidery/).
- **DTF printers** for full-color transfers on cotton, polyester and blends. See [DTF printing](/services/direct-to-film/).
- **DTG printers** for soft full-color prints on cotton. See [DTG printing](/services/direct-to-garment/).
- **A garment embossing machine** for raised logos on hoodies and sweatshirts. See [embossing](/services/embossing/).

## Since 2017

TBSP was started in Los Angeles in 2017 by brothers who wanted custom apparel to be easy to design, easy to order and printed right. Since then our presses have run work for Netflix, Adidas, Nike, YouTube, Google and Amazon Alexa, and just as often for a first merch drop, a school team or a fundraiser. Read [our story](/about-us/).

## How a job moves through the shop

1. **Art check.** Every file is reviewed for free, and you get a mockup to approve.
2. **Setup.** Screens are burned or your logo is digitized for embroidery.
3. **Production.** Your order runs on the press, embroidery machine or printer that fits the design.
4. **Quality check and packing.** Pieces are counted, folded and packed.
5. **Pickup or shipping.** Free pickup in Los Angeles, or shipping anywhere in the lower 48, free on orders of {{shipFreeFrom}} or more.
