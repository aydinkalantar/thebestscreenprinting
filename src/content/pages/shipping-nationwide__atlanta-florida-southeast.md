## What buyers in the Southeast ask us for

Since 2018, about 410 quote requests have come to us from Atlanta, Miami, Orlando, Fort Lauderdale, Charlotte, Raleigh, Nashville, Birmingham and Jackson. T-shirts first, then hoodies, hats and promo items, with screen printing and DTG the most-picked methods. Where people told us a quantity, the typical order was around 25 to 30 pieces.

Who orders: Churches and family reunions, college clubs and Greek life, companies, schools and teams, and music artists.

Family reunions, church events and Greek life are a big part of what the Southeast orders. See [college clubs and Greek life](/for/college-clubs-greek-life/) and [clubs and groups](/clubs-groups/).

## How it works from the Southeast

1. **Send your art and sizes** through the [quote form](/quick-quote/) or ask Max for an instant price.
2. **Approve the mockup** by email. The art check is free.
3. **We print it in-house** in Los Angeles or Cleveland.
4. **It ships to your door** with tracking. Free on orders of $300 or more, $35 below that.
