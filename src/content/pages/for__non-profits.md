## Wear the mission

A good cause shirt does three jobs. It makes volunteers easy to spot. It makes your event look organized and serious. And it keeps telling your story every time someone wears it afterward.

We print for Los Angeles non-profits, community groups and charity events from our shop at 2710 S Broadway Avenue in Downtown LA.

## What do non-profits order?

- **Volunteer shirts** in a bright color so your team stands out in a crowd
- **Walk, run and ride shirts** for participants, often with sponsor logos on the back
- **Fundraiser merch** to sell at events or online
- **Staff and board apparel:** [embroidered](/services/embroidery/) polos and jackets for galas and outreach
- **Awareness pieces** like tees, hats and totes for outreach and giveaways

## How do we stretch the budget?

- **Keep colors down.** In [screen printing](/screen-printing/), each ink color adds a screen. One or two colors keep costs low and still read clearly.
- **Order one design.** Quantity counts per design, so one shared design across volunteers reaches better price breaks.
- **Pick a solid basic tee.** Save premium blanks for merch you plan to sell.
- **Plan ahead.** Ordering early keeps you off the rush schedule and gives you more blank options.

## Small group or big event?

- **Under 24 pieces:** [direct-to-garment (DTG)](/services/direct-to-garment/) with no minimum and no setup fee, or [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/).
- **24 or more of a design:** screen printing, printed in-house.
- **Names on staff shirts:** [heat transfer](/services/heat-transfer-vinyl/).

Organizing a club or community group too? See [specialty shirts for clubs and groups](/clubs-groups/).
