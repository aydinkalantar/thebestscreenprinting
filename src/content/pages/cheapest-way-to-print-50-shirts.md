## Five ways to cut the price further

1. **Go to 72 pieces if you can.** A one-color tee drops to about {{tee.screen1.72}} each, so 72 shirts cost about {{$ tee.screen1.72 × 72 + screenSetup}}, only about {{$ tee.screen1.72 × 72 - tee.screen1.48 × 50}} more than 50.
2. **Use fewer ink colors.** Each extra color adds a {{screenSetup}} screen and about {{$. tee.screen2.48 - tee.screen1.48}} per shirt at this quantity.
3. **Keep it to one print spot.** If you want two, a small left-chest print with a large back print looks premium for the money.
4. **Pick a standard blank.** The Gildan 5000 and Bella+Canvas 3001 are the best value. Heavyweight or oversized tees cost more.
5. **Avoid rush.** Order about 2 weeks ahead and skip the {{rushPercent}}% to {{superRushPercent}}% rush fee.
