## Your print shop is on Holmes Avenue

Ordering shirts online from a print shop three states away means waiting on boxes, guessing at colors and hoping the tracking number moves. When the shop is in Los Angeles, you can skip most of that. TBSP is at 5738 Holmes Ave., open Monday through Friday from 9am to 5pm, and every screen printed order is printed under our own roof.

## Why does local matter for custom tees?

- **Pick up, don't wait.** Grab your order from the shop the moment it is ready instead of waiting on a carrier.
- **Talk blanks in person.** Work through shirt weights, fabrics and colors before you commit.
- **Talk it through.** Bring artwork, a sample shirt or just an idea, and work it out face to face.
- **Fix it fast.** Need to tweak a size breakdown or add a few pieces? A phone call reaches the people running your job.
- **Rush is realistic.** Normal (about 2 weeks), Rush (1 week, +15%) and Super Rush (3 business days, +30%) turnaround options are available, and being close means no shipping day to add on top.
