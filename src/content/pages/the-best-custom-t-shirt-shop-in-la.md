## The shirt matters as much as the print

Ink on a thin, boxy tee looks cheap. The same ink on a soft, well-cut shirt looks like retail. After putting screen prints, stitches and DTG on a lot of blanks, these are the ones we keep coming back to.

## Our top 10 T-shirts

1. **Bella + Canvas 3001 Unisex Staple Tee** (XS to 5XL). Soft, modern fit, huge color range. Our default recommendation for brands and merch.
2. **Comfort Colors 1717 Garment-Dyed Heavyweight Tee** (S to 4XL). Thick, relaxed and washed-in color. The vintage look without the thrift-store hunt.
3. **Gildan 5000 Classic Tee** (S to 5XL). The heavy cotton workhorse for events, teams and giveaways.
4. **Gildan 64000 Softstyle Tee** (S to 3XL). Lighter and softer than the 5000, still budget-friendly.
5. **Bella + Canvas 3413 Tri-Blend Tee** (XS to 4XL). Drapey, ultra-soft, a heathered look that makes water-based and discharge prints sing.
6. **Bella + Canvas 3001ECO Staple Eco Tee** (XS to 3XL). The 3001 fit with an eco-minded fabric.
7. **Bella + Canvas 3001U Staple Tee, made in the USA.** For brands that want domestic production.
8. **AS Colour 5082 Oversized Faded Tee.** Boxy streetwear fit with a faded finish.
9. **Bella + Canvas 3001T Toddler Tee** (2T to 5T). Match the whole family.
10. **Bella + Canvas 3001Y Youth Tee** (S to XL). For schools, camps and youth teams.

## How do you choose a blank?

- **Brand drop or retail?** Go soft and fitted: 3001, 3413 or the M2580 hoodie.
- **Event or giveaway?** Go durable and budget-friendly: 5000, 64000 or the 18500.
- **Vintage mood?** Comfort Colors 1717 or AS Colour 5082.
- **Whole family or school?** The 3001 in adult, youth and toddler sizes keeps everything matched.

Size ranges and colors vary by style and stock, so check the [catalog](/catalog/) for what is available now. Prefer another label? See the [brands we print on](/customizable-name-brand-apparel/).
