## The label inside matters

Your print gets all the attention, but the shirt underneath decides how it feels, fits and lasts. A soft, well-cut blank turns merch into something people wear on purpose. Here are the brands we customize most, and what each one does best.

## Soft, fitted, fashion-forward

**Bella + Canvas.** Modern cuts, a huge color range and the 3001 tee, one of the most-printed shirts in the business. Tri-blends, eco fabrics and made-in-USA options too.

**Next Level.** Lightweight, retail-style fits that feel great and print clean. A favorite for brands and events that want a polished look.

**American Apparel.** A long-time TBSP staff favorite for premium lightweight fabrics and a big selection of fitted tees.

## Washed, heavy and vintage

**Comfort Colors.** Garment-dyed for that soft, lived-in color. Heavy, relaxed and made for a vintage look.

**Champion.** Classic athletic heritage. Sweatshirts and hoodies with a recognizable logo on the sleeve.

## Durable everyday basics

**Gildan.** The workhorse. From the 5000 heavy cotton tee to the 18500 hoodie, it is the go-to for teams, events and bulk orders.

**Hanes.** Familiar comfort and reliable basics at friendly prices.

**Fruit of the Loom.** Simple, durable staples for large runs and giveaways.

**Jerzees.** Tough tees and fleece built for everyday wear.

**Anvil.** Lightweight basics that print well for promotional runs.

## Workwear and corporate

**District.** Contemporary tees and fleece with a fashion edge, popular for staff and corporate apparel.

For uniform pieces like polos and work shirts, see [business and polo styles](/products/business-and-polos-landings/).

## How do you choose the right brand?

- **Selling it?** Choose soft and fitted: Bella + Canvas, Next Level or American Apparel.
- **Going for vintage?** Comfort Colors.
- **Big event or giveaway?** Gildan, Hanes, Fruit of the Loom or Jerzees.
- **Embroidered staff wear?** District or a heavier blank that holds stitches well.
