## Merch that looks like you made it on purpose

Your followers can tell the difference between a logo slapped on a cheap tee and a piece that was designed. We help creators make the second kind: the right blank, the right method and a print that survives the wash.

## Test small, then scale

The smartest drops start with proof.

1. **Test it.** Print a 6-piece run with [direct-to-garment (DTG)](/services/direct-to-garment/). Shoot it, wear it, post it.
2. **Read the room.** See which colorway and design your audience actually wants.
3. **Scale it.** Once you need a bigger run of a design, move to [screen printing](/screen-printing/) for bold ink and a better per-piece price.

## What should my first drop include?

Keep it tight. Two or three pieces done well beat ten forgettable ones.

- **A hero tee.** Your strongest design on a quality [t-shirt](/products/t-shirts/).
- **A hoodie.** Higher value, worn for years. See [hoodies and sweatshirts](/products/sweatshirts-hoodies/).
- **A hat.** An [embroidered hat](/products/hats/) is an easy add-on with a stitched, premium finish.

Totes, towels and bags work well as giveaways and bundle extras.

## Finish options that stand out

- **Screen printing** with solid spot colors and specialty inks
- **Embroidery** for logos on hats, polos and jackets
- **[Direct-to-film](/services/direct-to-film/)** for crisp full-color detail on many fabrics
- **[Heat transfer](/services/heat-transfer-vinyl/)** for names and numbers
