## What buyers in the Northeast ask us for

Since 2018, about 200 quote requests have come to us from Boston, Philadelphia and its suburbs, Pittsburgh, Providence and Southern New Hampshire. T-shirts first, then a high share of hoodies and sweatshirts, then hats and polos. Where people told us a quantity, the typical order was around 22 to 25 pieces.

Who orders: Colleges and student groups, companies, sports teams, and breweries and restaurants.

With so many colleges in the Northeast, hoodies and crewnecks come up almost as often as tees. See [custom hoodies in bulk](/custom-hoodies-in-bulk/).

## How it works from the Northeast

1. **Send your art and sizes** through the [quote form](/quick-quote/) or ask Max for an instant price.
2. **Approve the mockup** by email. The art check is free.
3. **We print it in-house** in Los Angeles or Cleveland.
4. **It ships to your door** with tracking. Free on orders of $300 or more, $35 below that.
