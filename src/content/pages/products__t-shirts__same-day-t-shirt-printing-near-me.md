## Deadline today? Let's get you on press.

The event got moved up. The shoot is tomorrow. Someone forgot the shirts. We see it every week, and same-day and next-day rush slots exist for exactly that. What decides whether your job fits in one is mostly prep. Here is how to make your order rush-ready.

For slot availability, methods and how to book, see our main [same-day t-shirt printing in Los Angeles](/same-day-t-shirt-printing-los-angeles/) page. This page is your checklist.

## The rush-ready checklist

**1. Have final artwork.** Rush time disappears fastest on design changes. Send print-ready files: vector (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF) or a high-resolution PNG. Our free art check still happens, it just goes faster when the art is already clean.

**2. Pick a shirt we can get today.** Standard colors in common blanks are the safest bet. Specialty fabrics, unusual colors or hard-to-find sizes can push a job past the window. Ask us what is on hand.

**3. Lock the numbers.** Send a final size breakdown up front. Adding a few XLs halfway through costs more time than it looks like.

**4. Keep the print simple.** Fewer ink colors and fewer print locations mean fewer screens and fewer passes.

**5. Call first.** Before you build your day around a pickup time, call (323) 433-0033 so we can confirm the slot.

## What slows a rush order down?

- Artwork that still needs to be redrawn or vectorized
- Many ink colors across multiple locations
- Garments that are not in stock locally
- Late changes to quantities or sizes
- Methods with extra steps, such as embroidery digitizing

None of these are deal breakers on a normal schedule. On a rush, each one eats into the clock.

## Which method is fastest for my order?

It depends on quantity. For 24 or more of one design, [screen printing](/screen-printing/) is printed in-house and moves fast once screens are set. For smaller orders, [DTG](/services/direct-to-garment/) skips setup entirely, and [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/) is another quick small-run option. Need names on the back? [Heat transfer vinyl](/services/heat-transfer-vinyl/) adds them without a new screen.
