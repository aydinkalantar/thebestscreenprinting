## Minimums by order type

| Order type | Minimum |
| --- | --- |
| Screen printing, 1–3 ink colors | 6 pieces |
| Screen printing, 4–6 ink colors | 50 pieces |
| Screen printing, 7–10 ink colors | 100 pieces |
| DTF, full color | 6 pieces |
| DTG, full color | 6 pieces |
| Embroidery | 6 pieces |
| Finishings (printed tags, hem tags) | 50 pieces |
| Embossing | 24 pieces |
| CMYK process printing | 50 or 100 pieces |

The full rules are in our [terms and conditions](/terms-conditions/).

## The best method for a small order

For 6 to 23 pieces, the price per shirt is higher because setup is spread over fewer shirts. Two good options:

- **1-color design:** screen printing, about $18 per shirt at 6 pieces and $16 at 12, shirt included, plus a $25 screen.
- **Full-color design:** DTF, about $17 per shirt at 6 and $15 at 12, no setup fee.

From 24 pieces, prices drop quickly. See the [full price table](/how-much-does-custom-shirt-printing-cost/).

## Small orders still get the full treatment

A free art check, a mockup to approve, production in our own facilities and shipping to any US state, free from $300. Read how [nationwide shipping](/shipping-nationwide/) works.
