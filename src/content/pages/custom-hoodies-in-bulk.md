## How the price works

Your hoodie price is three parts:

1. **The blank.** Set by the brand and style, and a little more for 2XL and up.
2. **The decoration.** The same price grid as tees. It drops fast with quantity.
3. **Setup, if any.** $25 per screen for screen printing (white base on dark hoodies is free), $75 one-time digitizing for embroidery, none for DTF or DTG.

For a quick price, ask Max with your quantity and design. Max prices the exact hoodie with today's blank cost.

## Popular hoodie orders

- **Team and school hoodies:** front print, name or number on the back.
- **Company hoodies:** embroidered left-chest logo, sometimes with a printed back.
- **Brand drops:** heavyweight hoodies with large front and back prints and a sleeve hit.
- **Event and tour merch:** one bold design, fast turnaround with [Rush or Super Rush](/rush-custom-shirts-shipped-fast/).
