## How ordering from another state works

You never need to visit. Here is the whole process, start to finish:

1. **Send your idea.** Use the [quote form](/quick-quote/) or ask Max: garment, quantity, deadline and artwork. A phone photo of a sketch is fine.
2. **Get a price within one business day.** Your quote lists every print spot, the garment, the delivery option and the $35 shipping.
3. **Approve the mockup.** We check your art for free and send a mockup with placement and sizes. Nothing is printed until you approve it.
4. **We make it in-house.** Screen printing, embroidery, DTF or DTG in our own facilities, never sent out to another shop.
5. **It ships with tracking.** You get an email when it is approved, in production and shipped.

## When will it arrive?

Pick the delivery option that fits your date. The time includes production and shipping.

- **Normal:** arrives in about 2 weeks. No extra cost.
- **Rush:** arrives in 1 week. Adds 15% to the order.
- **Super Rush:** arrives in 3 business days. Adds 30%. Order by 2:00 PM ET Tuesday to have it by Friday. Not available for every product, style or PO box.

More detail on [delivery times](/learn-delivery-time/) and [rush orders](/rush-custom-shirts-shipped-fast/).

## Who orders from out of state

Teams, schools and college clubs, companies ordering staff shirts and polos, bands and touring artists, event organizers, churches and non-profits, and clothing brands launching a drop. If you order shirts more than once a year, we save your artwork and sizes so a reorder takes one email.

## Two facilities, one standard

Our [Los Angeles](/los-angeles-ca/) shop and our [Cleveland](/cleveland-oh/) facility use the same inks, threads and art check. Whichever one makes your order, you get the same mockup approval and the same reprint promise in our [shipping and returns policy](/shipping-and-delivery/).
