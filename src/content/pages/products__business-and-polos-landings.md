## Look like a team. Instantly.

Customers decide in seconds whether your business looks put together. A matching polo with a crisp, stitched logo on the chest handles that for you. It reads professional on the sales floor, at the host stand, behind the counter and at the trade show booth, and it looks just as good on day 200 as on day one.

## Why do businesses choose embroidered polos?

- **Polished, not stiff.** More dressed-up than a tee, more comfortable than a button-down.
- **Built to last.** Thread does not fade, crack or peel, even through regular washing.
- **Feels premium.** A stitched logo has texture and depth that signals quality.
- **Works everywhere.** Office, retail, restaurant, golf course, conference floor.

[Embroidery](/services/embroidery/) is our main method for polos, hats, jackets and uniforms.

## Where to put your logo

- **Left chest:** the classic. Clean, balanced, instantly recognizable.
- **Sleeve:** a second mark, a tagline or a department name.
- **Upper back:** your website or brand name for teams working events.
- **Right chest:** the employee's name or title, embroidered or added with [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/).

## Getting your logo stitch-ready

A logo built for a screen does not always translate straight to thread. Very small text and hairline details can blur when stitched. Our free art check reviews your logo, flags anything that needs simplifying and confirms the size before production. Send vector artwork if you have it.

Brand colors matter, too. Tell us yours and we will pick thread colors to match as closely as possible.
