## What buyers in the Midwest ask us for

Since 2018, about 430 quote requests have come to us from Chicago and its suburbs, Columbus, Cleveland, Detroit, St. Louis, Milwaukee, Minneapolis and Indianapolis. T-shirts first, then a big share of hoodies and sweatshirts, then hats and promo items. Where people told us a quantity, the typical order was around 24 pieces.

Who orders: Companies and schools, sports teams and rec leagues, breweries and restaurants, and bands on tour.

Our second production facility is in [Cleveland, Ohio](/cleveland-oh/), right in the Midwest. Hoodie season is long here, so see [custom hoodies in bulk](/custom-hoodies-in-bulk/) for blanks and prices.

## How it works from the Midwest

1. **Send your art and sizes** through the [quote form](/quick-quote/) or ask Max for an instant price.
2. **Approve the mockup** by email. The art check is free.
3. **We print it in-house** in Los Angeles or Cleveland.
4. **It ships to your door** with tracking. Free on orders of $300 or more, $35 below that.
