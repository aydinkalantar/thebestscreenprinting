## Heavy fleece. Loud prints. Clean stitches.

A tee gets worn for a season. A good hoodie gets worn for years. That is why brands build drops around them, companies hand them out to the team, and crews live in them on set. We decorate hoodies out of our Los Angeles shop, in classic and oversized fits from XS to 5XL.

## Four ways to make it yours

**Screen printing.** Thick, opaque ink that sits bold on heavy fleece and survives wash after wash. Our go-to for bold designs and bigger runs, from 12 hoodies. [See screen printing](/screen-printing/).

**Embroidery.** Thread instead of ink, for a raised, premium finish. A stitched chest logo turns a basic hoodie into something that feels retail. [See embroidery](/services/embroidery/).

**Direct-to-garment.** Full-color artwork printed straight into the fabric, from 6 pieces. Ideal for photo-heavy and full-color designs. [See DTG](/services/direct-to-garment/).

**Heat transfer.** Crisp names, numbers and small text, often layered on top of a screen printed design. [See heat transfer vinyl](/services/heat-transfer-vinyl/).

Mixing methods is normal. A screen printed back with an embroidered chest logo is one of the best-looking hoodies you can make.

## Where can the design go?

Hoodies give you more real estate than a tee:

- **Left chest** for a small logo or wordmark
- **Full front** for the statement piece
- **Full back** for the big graphic
- **Sleeves** for a tagline, date or secondary logo
- **Hood** for a detail people notice second

Every added location means another print or stitch run, so pick the ones that earn their spot.

## Who orders custom hoodies?

- **Clothing brands and artists** building merch with real weight to it
- **Companies** outfitting teams with gear they will actually wear
- **Schools, clubs and teams** ordering for the season
- **Production crews and events** that run cold early in the morning

## Picking the right blank

Fleece weight, fabric blend and fit all change how a hoodie prints and feels. Cotton-rich fleece takes ink and DTG beautifully; polyester-heavy blends can be better suited to other methods.
