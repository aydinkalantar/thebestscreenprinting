## How do you pick the right method?

Artwork is the first filter, and every order starts at 12 pieces. For bold designs with a few solid colors, [screen printing](/screen-printing/) is usually best, with the best price on bigger runs. For full-color photo art, [DTG](/services/direct-to-garment/) or [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/). For logos on polos, hats and jackets, [embroidery](/services/embroidery/). Compare all six on our [services overview](/services/).
