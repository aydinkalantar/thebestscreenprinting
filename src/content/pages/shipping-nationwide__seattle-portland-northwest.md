## What buyers in the Northwest ask us for

Since 2018, about 140 quote requests have come to us from Seattle, Portland, Spokane, Western Washington and Boise. T-shirts first, then hoodies and hats, with screen printing the most-picked method. Where people told us a quantity, the typical order was around 40 pieces.

Who orders: Companies and tech teams, breweries and coffee shops, bands, and outdoor and sports groups.

Rain and cold make hoodies, crewnecks and beanies popular here. Browse [hoodies](/catalog/category/hoodies/) and [beanies](/catalog/category/beanies/).

## How it works from the Northwest

1. **Send your art and sizes** through the [quote form](/quick-quote/) or ask Max for an instant price.
2. **Approve the mockup** by email. The art check is free.
3. **We print it in-house** in Los Angeles or Cleveland.
4. **It ships to your door** with tracking. Free on orders of {{shipFreeFrom}} or more, {{shipFlat}} below that.
