## Your name, on the thing they reach for

A towel is not decoration. It is something people actually need, every day, and that is exactly why a branded one works. Here is how different businesses across Los Angeles put them to use.

## Gyms and fitness studios

A logo towel on every bench makes a studio feel premium. Hand them out with memberships, sell them at the front desk or keep a stack for classes. A clean embroidered mark holds up to the constant wash cycle.

## Hotels, spas and salons

Monogrammed bath and hand towels quietly signal attention to detail. A small corner logo in a tone that matches the towel looks upscale without shouting.

## Sports teams and schools

Rally towels turn a crowd into a wall of color. Teams use them on the sideline; schools sell them for game days and graduations. Pair them with [team uniforms and jerseys](/products/team-uniforms-jerseys/).

## Events and tournaments

Golf outings, charity runs, festivals and pool parties. A towel in the welcome bag gets used that day and long after.

## Brands and merch

Beach towels and gym towels are strong merch add-ons, and a big, bold design turns a towel into a product people buy for its own sake.
