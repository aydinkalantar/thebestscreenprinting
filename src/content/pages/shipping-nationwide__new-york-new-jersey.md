## What buyers in New York ask us for

Since 2018, about 420 quote requests have come to us from New York City, Long Island, Westchester, Central and North Jersey and Southwest Connecticut. T-shirts lead by far, then hoodies and hats, and band, brand and fashion merch shows up often. Where people told us a quantity, the typical order was around 50 pieces.

Who orders: Clothing brands and designers, bands and artists, schools, non-profits and companies with offices in Manhattan and Brooklyn.

Fashion and streetwear labels in New York often order a short 24-piece test run on a [heavyweight tee](/oversized-heavyweight-t-shirt-printing/), then reorder the styles that sell.

## How it works from New York

1. **Send your art and sizes** through the [quote form](/quick-quote/) or ask Max for an instant price.
2. **Approve the mockup** by email. The art check is free.
3. **We print it in-house** in Los Angeles or Cleveland.
4. **It ships to your door** with tracking. Free on orders of {{shipFreeFrom}} or more, {{shipFlat}} below that.
