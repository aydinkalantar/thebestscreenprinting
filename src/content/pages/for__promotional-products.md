## Promo that does not end up in the trash

Cheap swag gets tossed. A soft tee, a good hat or a sturdy tote gets used for years, and your brand goes with it. We print promotional apparel and accessories for marketing teams, agencies and businesses, with the same care we give a fashion brand.

Our presses have run work for Netflix, Adidas, Nike, YouTube, Google, Amazon Alexa, Optimum Nutrition, 99 Cents Only and BCG Digital Ventures.

## What can we brand?

- **T-shirts and tanks:** the highest-reach giveaway. See [t-shirts](/products/t-shirts/).
- **Hoodies and sweatshirts:** premium gifts for clients and teams. See [hoodies and sweatshirts](/products/sweatshirts-hoodies/).
- **Hats:** embroidered caps and beanies. See [hats](/products/hats/).
- **Bags and totes:** practical and highly visible. See [bags](/products/bags/).
- **Towels:** for gyms, beach events and hospitality. See [towels](/products/towels/).
- **Polos and outerwear** for staff at events and activations

Browse blanks in the [catalog](/catalog/).

## Which method fits a promo run?

- **[Screen printing](/screen-printing/)** for bold, few-color designs: bold color and the best per-piece value, printed in-house.
- **[Embroidery](/services/embroidery/)** for hats, polos and jackets that feel high-end.
- **[DTG](/services/direct-to-garment/)** for full-color and photo art, from 12 pieces.
- **[DTF](/services/direct-to-film/)** for detailed logos on fabrics that are hard to print directly.
- **[Large-format screen printing](/services/large-format-screen-printing/)** for oversized statement prints.

## How do I plan a promo order?

1. **Know the audience.** Event attendees, clients or staff: each calls for a different item.
2. **Set the in-hands date** and work backward.
3. **Send vector art** if you have it. Every order gets a free art check.
4. **Approve the mockup**, then we print.
5. **Pick up or ship.** Collect at 2710 S Broadway Avenue in Downtown LA, or have it shipped.
