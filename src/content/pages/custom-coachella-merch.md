## Made for the desert

Coachella is a music festival, a runway and a brand stage all at once. The merch has to work in 100-degree afternoons and cold desert nights, and it has to photograph well. We print it all in Downtown LA, a straight shot down the 10 from the Coachella Valley.

## What festival merch works best?

- **Tees and tank tops.** Light and breathable for hot days. The core of any festival drop. See [t-shirts](/products/t-shirts/).
- **Embroidered hats and snapbacks.** Shade plus a stitched logo that lasts long after the weekend. See [hats](/products/hats/).
- **Hoodies and crewnecks.** For the after-dark sets when the temperature drops. See [hoodies and sweatshirts](/products/sweatshirts-hoodies/).
- **Totes and bags.** Carry the essentials, carry the logo.
- **Bandanas and scarves.** Dust protection with a print on it.
- **Retro and tie-dye looks.** Bold color that fits the festival mood.

## Who orders Coachella merch?

- **Artists and performers:** fan merch to sell, plus gear for the band and crew.
- **Brands and sponsors:** giveaways and staff shirts for activations.
- **Festival vendors:** uniforms and branded accessories for the team.
- **Creators:** pieces to wear and shoot all weekend. See [merch for influencers](/influencers/).
- **Friend groups and fan crews:** matching fits for the whole camp.

## Which print method should I choose?

- **24+ pieces of one design:** [screen printing](/screen-printing/). Solid, saturated ink, printed in-house.
- **Small runs or full-color art:** [DTG](/services/direct-to-garment/) with no minimum, or [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/).
- **Logos on hats and jackets:** [embroidery](/services/embroidery/).
- **Names on crew shirts:** [heat transfer](/services/heat-transfer-vinyl/).

Choose lighter colors and breathable fabrics for daytime pieces. Light-colored garments stay cooler in the sun, and large, heavy-ink prints breathe less than a soft print.

1. **Send your design** or work through an idea with us.
