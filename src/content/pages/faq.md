faqs:
  - q: "Is there a minimum order?"
    a: "Yes. Screen printing and embroidery start at 12 pieces, DTG, DTF and heat transfer at 6, and every order is produced in-house at our own facilities in Los Angeles and Cleveland."
  - q: "What determines the price of my order?"
    a: "Mainly four things: the garment you choose, the number of ink colors, the number of print locations and the total quantity. Personalization and samples can also affect the price."
  - q: "How can I lower the cost per item?"
    a: "Increase the quantity, or reduce the number of ink colors in your design. Our team can adapt most designs to one, two or three colors."
  - q: "How do I get a quote, and how fast will I hear back?"
    a: "Use the quick quote form, email sales@tbsp.la or call (323) 433-0033. We answer quotes within one business day, with no obligation to order."
  - q: "Can I cancel or change my order after approving the mockup?"
    a: "Please make changes during the mockup stage. Because every order is custom, there are no cancellations or refunds once a mockup is approved for production. If you need a change after approval, tell us right away, but we cannot guarantee it."
  - q: "Do you check my artwork before printing?"
    a: "Yes. Every order gets a free art check, and we send a mockup for your approval before production."
  - q: "Can you help create or fix my artwork?"
    a: "Yes. We offer graphic design help for creating or editing artwork. Email sales@tbsp.la with details about what you need."
  - q: "Which decoration methods do you offer?"
    a: "Screen printing, embroidery, DTG (direct to garment), DTF (direct to film) and heat transfer vinyl."
  - q: "What is embroidery best for?"
    a: "Polos, hats, jackets and uniforms, where a stitched logo gives a durable, finished look."
  - q: "How do you add names and numbers?"
    a: "With heat transfer, so each piece in the order can carry a different name or number."
  - q: "Can you rush my order?"
    a: "Normal (about 2 weeks), Rush (1 week, +15%) and Super Rush (3 business days, +30%) turnaround options are available. Availability depends on the garment, method and shop schedule, so confirm by phone or in your quote."
  - q: "Can I pick up my order?"
    a: "Yes. Our shop is at 5738 Holmes Ave., Los Angeles, CA 90058, open Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm."
  - q: "Do you ship orders?"
    a: "Yes. Tell us your delivery address when you request a quote, and we will include shipping options for your order."
