## Pick your stitch

Embroidery is not one look. It is a whole toolkit. The style you choose changes how your logo catches light, how it feels in the hand and how it reads from across the street. Here is what we stitch in Los Angeles, and when each one shines.

## Flat embroidery

Thread stitched directly into the fabric surface, using satin and running stitches for clean edges and fill. It is the classic: refined on polos, sharp on caps, at home on uniforms, towels and totes. If you are not sure, start here.

**Best for:** corporate logos, left-chest marks, text, detailed shapes.

## 3D puff embroidery

Foam goes down first, then thread stitches over it, so the design rises off the garment. Bold, sculpted and instantly recognizable on the front of a cap.

**Best for:** hat fronts, chunky lettering, bold logos. Skip it for thin lines and small type.

## Appliqué

Fabric cutouts are layered onto the garment and stitched around the edges. You get contrasting colors and textures, and big designs without a massive stitch count.

**Best for:** varsity letters, large chest or back designs, playful texture.

## Patches

Your design stitched as a standalone patch, then attached to the garment. Patches give a clean, finished edge and work on items that are tricky to stitch directly.

**Best for:** jackets, bags, hats, workwear.

## Chenille

The fuzzy, looped texture of a letterman jacket. Soft, nostalgic and loud in the best way.

**Best for:** varsity looks, bold initials, statement pieces.

## Monogram

Initials or short names in a clean script or block letter. Small, personal and polished.

**Best for:** gifts, staff names, robes and towels.
