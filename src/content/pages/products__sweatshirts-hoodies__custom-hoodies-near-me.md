## Hoodies are a touch-it product

Fleece weight, the hand of the fabric, how the hood sits, whether the fit is classic or oversized: you can read about all of it, but you understand it in about two seconds once it is in your hands. That is the case for a local shop. TBSP is at 2710 S Broadway Avenue in Downtown LA, and you can come talk it through in person.

## Why order hoodies from a shop down the street?

- **Talk blanks in person.** Work through fabric weight, fit and color with us instead of guessing from a photo.
- **Settle the design in one conversation.** Bring your art, a sample hoodie you love or a rough idea, and leave with a plan.
- **Pick up on your schedule.** Collect your order Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm, with no shipping window to wait out.
- **Real rush options.** Normal (about 2 weeks), Rush (1 week, +15%) and Super Rush (3 business days, +30%) turnaround options are available. Local pickup means no carrier day stacked on top.
- **One call reaches the shop floor.** Questions go to the people working on your job.

## What should I bring to the shop?

To make the visit count, bring:

- Your artwork, ideally as a vector file or high-resolution image
- A rough quantity and size breakdown
- Any reference hoodie whose fit or fabric you want to match
- Your deadline

No art yet? Bring the idea. We will talk through what prints well on fleece, and every order gets a free art check before production.

## LA hoodies, LA reasons

Los Angeles runs on hoodies. Early call times on set, cold nights at outdoor shows, beach mornings, studio sessions, office air conditioning. We decorate them for clothing brands, artists, production crews, event teams, schools and companies across the city, and we have worked with brands like Netflix, YouTube and Google.
