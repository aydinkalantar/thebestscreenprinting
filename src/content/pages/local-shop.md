## Screen printing, embroidery, DTG and DTF, in one DTLA building

This is where it happens. Screens get burned, ink gets mixed, needles fly and finished orders stack up for pickup, all at 2710 S Broadway in Downtown Los Angeles. You can order online, but there is something to be said for walking in and seeing your shirt before it ships.

## What you can do at the shop

- **Talk it through.** Bring a sketch, a file or just an idea. We will help you choose the garment, the method and the placement.
- **See colors in person.** Ink and thread look different on a screen. Hold them up to your garment.
- **Drop off your garments.** We print and stitch on customer-supplied shirts, hats and jackets. See [printing on your shirts](/services/custom-screen-printing-los-angeles/screen-printing-on-your-shirts/).
- **Pick up your order.** No shipping wait, no extra cost.

## What should you bring?

- Your design file, ideally vector, on a laptop or sent ahead by email
- A rough quantity and size breakdown
- Your deadline
- Any garments you want us to print on

## What we make here

[Screen printing](/screen-printing/) for bold designs and bigger runs, printed in-house. [Embroidery](/services/embroidery/) for hats, polos, jackets and uniforms. [DTG](/services/direct-to-garment/) for full-color and photo art. [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/) for full color on cotton, blends and polyester. [Heat transfer](/services/heat-transfer-vinyl/) for names and numbers. Plus [embossing](/services/embossing/) and [large format](/services/large-format-screen-printing/) prints.

## How do rush orders work at the shop?

Normal (about 2 weeks), Rush (1 week, +15%) and Super Rush (3 business days, +30%) turnaround options are available. Call before you come in so we can check press time and garment stock, then confirm your pickup time. More on [Rush and Super Rush printing](/same-day-t-shirt-printing-los-angeles/).

## Not in DTLA?

We ship across the continental US and can split orders across several addresses. See [fulfillment](/fulfillment/). Comparing local shops? Our checklist on [screen printing near me](/services/custom-screen-printing-los-angeles/screen-printing-near-me/) covers what to ask.
