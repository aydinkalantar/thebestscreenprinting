## The seven questions to ask any LA print shop

Los Angeles has hundreds of screen printers, from garage shops to big contract printers. These questions sort them out fast. Under each one is how we answer it, so you have something to compare against.

### 1. What is the all-in price per shirt?

Ask for one number that includes the blank shirt, the ink and the print. Some quotes leave out the shirt or the screens, which makes them look cheaper than they are.

### 2. What are the setup fees?

Screen printing needs one screen per ink color per print spot. Embroidery needs your logo digitized once. Ask for these up front. Ours are {{screenSetup}} per screen and {{digitizing}} per embroidery design.

### 3. What is the minimum order?

Ask the minimum for your method, not just the shop's lowest number. Ours is 12 pieces for screen printing and embroidery, and 6 for [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/) and [DTG](/services/direct-to-garment/). Read more on [minimum orders](/custom-shirts-minimum-order/).

### 4. Can you hit my date?

Give the shop the day you need the shirts in your hands, and ask whether that date is guaranteed. Our options:

- **Normal:** about 2 weeks.
- **Rush:** 1 week, {{rushPercent}}% more.
- **Super Rush:** 3 business days, {{superRushPercent}}% more, on orders up to {{superRushMaxPieces}} pieces.

### 5. Do I see a proof before you print?

You should get a free art check and a mockup to approve, showing print size, placement and ink colors. We send one on every order, and nothing is printed until you approve it.

### 6. What happens if something is wrong?

Ask what the shop does if a print is crooked, the wrong size or the wrong count. If we get the size count, placement or art size wrong, we reprint it at no charge. Report any problem within 24 hours of delivery. See our [shipping and returns policy](/shipping-and-delivery/).

A shop you can visit makes it easy to check a sample or drop off your own garments. Ours is at 5738 Holmes Ave., Los Angeles, CA 90058, just south of Downtown, open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm. Pickup is free. See our [Los Angeles shop page](/los-angeles-ca/).

## Red flags when comparing quotes

- **No blank shirt in the price.** The shirt is often half the cost.
- **No proof.** You find out about a mistake when the box arrives.
- **"Rush" with no date.** Ask for an in-hand date in writing.
- **Very low minimums for screen printing.** Screens cost the same for 5 shirts or 500, so a tiny screen print run is either expensive or not really screen printed. For under 12 pieces, DTF or DTG is the honest answer.
- **No reviews.** Read the shop's recent Google reviews, not just the star rating, and look at how the owner replies.

## Which method should I ask for?

- **1 to 3 color logo or text, 24+ pieces:** [screen printing](/screen-printing/), the cheapest per shirt.
- **Full-color art or a photo, or under 24 pieces:** [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/).
- **Polos, caps, jackets and workwear:** [embroidery](/services/embroidery/).
