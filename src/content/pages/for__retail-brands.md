Clothing labels, streetwear brands, artists, creators, companies with merch to move. We print for all of them out of our shop at 5738 Holmes Ave. in Los Angeles, where every order, from 6 pieces (12 for screen printing and embroidery) up, is printed in-house. The list of brands we have printed for includes Netflix, Adidas, Nike, YouTube, Google, Amazon Alexa, Optimum Nutrition, 99 Cents Only and BCG Digital Ventures.

## What do brands print with us?

- Graphic tees and long sleeves for a collection or a drop
- Hoodies, crewnecks and sweatpants
- Embroidered hats, beanies and jacket logos
- Tote bags and accessories
- Creator and channel merch
- Branded gear for in-store staff

Pick your blanks by style and color in our [catalog](/catalog/).

## Which decoration methods do you offer?

- **[Screen printing](/screen-printing/)** for production runs and bold, few-color designs. Heavy, opaque ink with a hand-pulled feel, printed in-house.
- **[Embroidery](/services/embroidery/)** for hats, polos, jackets and small chest logos with raised, textured thread.
- **[Direct to film](/services/direct-to-film/)** and DTG for full-color art, gradients and photo detail.
- **[Heat transfer vinyl](/services/heat-transfer-vinyl/)** for names, numbers and piece-by-piece personalization.

The best collections mix them. Think a screen-printed front graphic with a matching embroidered cap.

## How should I plan a drop?

Test first, then scale. Start with a short run of 6 pieces (12 for screen printing and embroidery) to check fit, color and print placement. Once the design is locked, scale up to the full production run, with screen printing for bold, few-color designs and the best price on bigger runs.

Get us your art early. Every file gets a free art check, and you approve a mockup before production. That is where you dial in size, placement and ink colors. Fewer colors usually means a lower cost per piece.

## Art not print-ready yet?

Send what you have. We offer graphic design help for creating or editing artwork, so a rough file can still become a clean print.
