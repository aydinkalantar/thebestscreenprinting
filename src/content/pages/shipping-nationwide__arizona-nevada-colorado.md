## What buyers in the Southwest ask us for

Since 2018, about 220 quote requests have come to us from Phoenix, Tucson, Las Vegas, Reno, Denver, Colorado Springs and Salt Lake City. T-shirts first, then hoodies and hats, with screen printing and DTG the most-picked methods. Where people told us a quantity, the typical order was around 20 pieces.

Who orders: Trade show and convention exhibitors in Las Vegas, companies, sports teams and events.

Las Vegas trade shows and conventions often run on tight dates. See [rush orders](/rush-custom-shirts-shipped-fast/) and [promotional products](/for/promotional-products/).

## How it works from the Southwest

1. **Send your art and sizes** through the [quote form](/quick-quote/) or ask Max for an instant price.
2. **Approve the mockup** by email. The art check is free.
3. **We print it in-house** in Los Angeles or Cleveland.
4. **It ships to your door** with tracking. Free on orders of $300 or more, $35 below that.
