## What changes the price

- **Quantity.** The biggest factor. Going from 24 to 72 shirts cuts the screen print price by about 30%.
- **Number of ink colors.** Screen printing adds a screen and a pass per color. Full-color art is often cheaper as DTF or DTG.
- **Number of print spots.** A back print or sleeve print is priced as its own spot. See [front, back and sleeve printing](/front-back-sleeve-printing/).
- **The garment.** Heavyweight, oversized, organic and name-brand blanks cost more than a basic tee. Max prices any shirt in our [catalog](/catalog/) with its real blank price.
- **Delivery date.** Rush adds 15% and Super Rush adds 30%.

## How to get the lowest price

1. Order everything at once instead of in small batches.
2. Keep screen printed designs to 1 to 3 colors.
3. Use one print spot, or a small left-chest print plus a big back print.
4. Pick a standard blank like the Gildan 5000 or Bella+Canvas 3001.
5. Give us your date early so you don't need Rush.

More tips in [the cheapest way to print 50 shirts](/cheapest-way-to-print-50-shirts/).

## Which method should you pick?

Short version: screen printing for 24 or more shirts with simple colors, DTF or DTG for small runs and photos, embroidery for polos, hats and jackets. The full comparison is in [screen printing vs DTF vs DTG](/screen-printing-vs-dtf-vs-dtg/).
