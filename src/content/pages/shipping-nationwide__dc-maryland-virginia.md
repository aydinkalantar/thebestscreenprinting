## What buyers in the DC area ask us for

Since 2018, about 160 quote requests have come to us from Washington DC, the Maryland suburbs, Baltimore, Northern Virginia, Richmond and Virginia Beach. T-shirts first, then hoodies and jackets, with a mix of screen printing, DTG and promo products. Where people told us a quantity, the typical order was around 25 pieces.

Who orders: Non-profits and associations, government contractors and companies, schools and churches, and event organizers.

Associations and non-profits around DC order a lot of event and volunteer shirts. See [shirts for non-profits](/for/non-profits/) and [company merch](/for/company-merch/).

## How it works from the DC area

1. **Send your art and sizes** through the [quote form](/quick-quote/) or ask Max for an instant price.
2. **Approve the mockup** by email. The art check is free.
3. **We print it in-house** in Los Angeles or Cleveland.
4. **It ships to your door** with tracking. Free on orders of $300 or more, $35 below that.
