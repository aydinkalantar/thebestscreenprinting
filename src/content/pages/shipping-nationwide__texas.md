## What buyers in Texas ask us for

Since 2018, about 250 quote requests have come to us from Houston, Dallas, Austin, Oklahoma City and New Orleans. T-shirts first, then hoodies and hats, with screen printing the most-picked method. Where people told us a quantity, the typical order was around 20 pieces.

Who orders: Companies, churches, schools and teams, food trucks and restaurants, and events and festivals.

Texas summers are hot, so many orders here go on lighter tees and performance shirts. For polyester performance fabric, [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/) is the safest choice.

## How it works from Texas

1. **Send your art and sizes** through the [quote form](/quick-quote/) or ask Max for an instant price.
2. **Approve the mockup** by email. The art check is free.
3. **We print it in-house** in Los Angeles or Cleveland.
4. **It ships to your door** with tracking. Free on orders of $300 or more, $35 below that.
