## Normal: Standard Delivery

Not in a hurry? Standard Delivery is estimated to arrive in **about 2 weeks**. It adds nothing to your order cost.

Normal orders get the same mockup approval, free art check and quality checks as every TBSP order. Everything is made in-house in Los Angeles and Cleveland, and every order starts at 12 pieces.

## Rush: One-Week Rush Delivery

Launch next week? **One-Week Rush Delivery** arrives at your doorstep in **1 week** and adds **15%** to the order cost.

## Super Rush: 3-Day Super Rush Delivery

Event this weekend? **3-Day Super Rush Delivery** arrives in **3 business days** and adds **30%** to the order cost. Order by **2:00 PM ET on Tuesday** to have items in hand by Friday. Super Rush is not available for all products, styles or PO Box addresses.

What helps a rush order go smoothly:

- **Garment stock.** Common blanks in common colors move fastest.
- **Method.** [DTG](/services/direct-to-garment/) and [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/) need no screens. [Screen printing](/screen-printing/) needs screens made first. Every order starts at 12 pieces.
- **Approved art.** The sooner you approve the mockup, the sooner we print.

Call (323) 433-0033 before you commit to a deadline, or read more on [Rush and Super Rush printing](/same-day-t-shirt-printing-los-angeles/).

## Can I pick up my order in Los Angeles?

Yes. Local pickup at 2710 S Broadway Avenue, Los Angeles, CA 90007 skips transit time entirely. Pickup hours are Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

## How to hit your date

1. **Start with a quote.** Quotes are answered within one business day.
2. **Send print-ready art.** See our [design tips](/helpful-design-tips/).
3. **Approve the mockup quickly.** Production starts after approval.
4. **Tell us your in-hands date.** Not just the ship date: the day you actually need it.

Shipping, reorders and returns are covered on [shipping and delivery](/shipping-and-delivery/).

Have a deadline? [Get a free quote](/quick-quote/) and put the date in the notes.
