## What makes screen printed tees worth it?

Screen printing lays down a thick, opaque layer of ink for each color in your design. That gives you:

- **Punchy, saturated color** that pops even on black and dark shirts
- **A print that lasts** through repeated washing and wearing
- **Better value per shirt** as your quantity climbs, because the screens are set up once

It is the go-to for brands, artists, events, teams and staff uniforms, starting at 12 shirts.

## What affects the price of screen printed shirts?

No price list fits every job, but these are the levers that move your quote:

- **Quantity.** Setup is a one-time cost, so it spreads thinner over bigger runs.
- **Number of ink colors.** Each color needs its own screen and its own pass on press.
- **Print locations.** Front, back, sleeve and neck each add a separate print.
- **The shirt itself.** A basic cotton tee and a premium heavyweight blank cost different amounts.

Want to keep the price tight? Fewer colors and fewer locations go a long way. Our free art check will also point out where a design can be simplified without losing its punch.
