## Built for people whose name is the brand

When your name is on the shirt, the shirt has to be right. Color matched. Placement exact. Fabric that people want to keep wearing. We work with talent, managers, assistants and creative teams who need premium custom apparel without the runaround.

## What do talent teams order?

- **Fan merch** for drops, shows and online stores
- **Tour and crew gear:** tees, hoodies and hats for the team behind the scenes
- **Wrap and premiere shirts** for cast and crew
- **Gifts:** embroidered jackets, hats and polos for collaborators
- **Event pieces** for launches, parties and brand appearances

## Pick the finish, not just the shirt

- **[Screen printing](/screen-printing/)** for 24 or more pieces of one design. Rich, solid ink and specialty effects, printed in-house.
- **[Embroidery](/services/embroidery/)** for a stitched, elevated look on [hats](/products/hats/), polos and jackets.
- **[Direct-to-garment](/services/direct-to-garment/)** for photo-real or full-color art in small runs, with no minimum and no setup fee.
- **[Direct-to-film](/services/direct-to-film/)** for crisp detail on fabrics and spots that are hard to print directly.

Need help choosing? Tell us the look and we will recommend a method.

## How do we keep big-name orders on track?

- **Mockups for approval.** Every order gets a mockup with dimensions, placements, sizing and style details before anything prints.
- **Free art check.** We review every file before it goes on press.
- **Quality checks.** Our team checks orders before they leave the shop.
- **Fast answers.** Quotes come back within one business day.

Our presses have printed for brands like Netflix, Adidas, Nike, YouTube and Google, so we know what a high-stakes deadline feels like.

## Working together

Respond quickly at the mockup stage and your timeline holds. Delays in approval push the completion date back, so we keep the back-and-forth short and clear.
