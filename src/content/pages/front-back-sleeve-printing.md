## Print spots and their sizes

| Spot | Largest size | Common use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Front | 12" × 14" | Main design |
| Back | 12" × 14" | Big design, names, numbers, tour dates |
| Left chest | 4" × 4" | Logo, often embroidered |
| Right chest | 4" × 4" | Second logo, name |
| Left or right sleeve | 3.5" × 3.5" | Small logo, flag, website |
| Back neck | 3.5" × 2" | Brand mark, small logo |
| Hat front | 4.5" × 2.25" | Embroidered logo |

## What a second spot adds

One-color screen print on a second spot, per shirt, plus a $25 screen per color:

| Quantity | Added per shirt |
| --- | --- |
| 6–11 | About $5.00 |
| 12–23 | About $4.50 |
| 24 | About $3.50 |
| 48 | About $2.75 |
| 72 | About $2.25 |
| 144 | About $1.75 |

A second DTG spot on a dark shirt is about $14 at 6 pieces down to $6.50 at 144. Embroidery spots are priced by stitch count. See the [full price table](/how-much-does-custom-shirt-printing-cost/).

## Popular combinations

- **Company shirt:** embroidered left-chest logo, screen printed back.
- **Band or tour tee:** big front design, tour dates on the back.
- **Brand drop:** front logo, back graphic, back-neck mark and a sleeve hit.
- **Team shirt:** front team name, back name and number.
