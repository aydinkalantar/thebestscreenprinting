## Make your budget go further

1. **Collect sizes first** and order everything at once.
2. **Keep it to 1 to 3 ink colors** for screen printing.
3. **Hit $300** and shipping is free. Most orders of 24 or more shirts do.
4. **Order early.** Normal delivery takes about 2 weeks; [Rush and Super Rush](/rush-custom-shirts-shipped-fast/) cost 15% to 30% more.
