## Design it like you mean it

Anyone can center a logo on a sweatshirt. Making one people reach for every morning comes down to a few choices: the color of the garment, the ink or thread, where the design sits and how big it is. Here is how to make each call.

## Start with the garment color

The sweatshirt color sets the mood before the design does.

- **Tone-on-tone** (black on charcoal, cream on oatmeal) looks quiet and expensive.
- **High contrast** (white on black, gold on navy) reads from across a room.
- **Brand color** garments turn the whole sweatshirt into the logo.

Light inks on dark fleece need an opaque method. [Screen printing](/screen-printing/) and [DTF](/services/direct-to-film/) handle that well.

## Ink or thread?

**Ink** covers ground. Big graphics, full backs and detailed artwork belong to [screen printing](/screen-printing/) or [DTG](/services/direct-to-garment/).

**Thread** adds texture and a premium feel. [Embroidery](/services/embroidery/) shines on small logos and wordmarks on the chest or sleeve. Very fine lines and tiny text do not stitch as cleanly as they print, so keep embroidered details bold.

## Where should the design go on a sweatshirt?

- **Left chest:** small and sharp, the corporate classic
- **Center chest:** the statement, often arched or stacked
- **Full back:** the canvas for the big graphic
- **Sleeve:** a date, location, tagline or second logo
- **Lower hem or cuff:** a subtle detail for brand pieces

Scale matters as much as placement. A design that looks right on screen can feel tiny on an adult XL and huge on a youth size. We check sizing during the art check.

## Make every piece personal

One design, many owners. Add first names, numbers or graduation years with [heat transfer vinyl](/services/heat-transfer-vinyl/) on top of a shared print. Clubs, teams and staff love it.

## Prep your artwork

- Send vector files (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF) or high-resolution images
- Convert text to outlines, or tell us the font
- List your brand colors
- Note each print location and the size you have in mind

Every order gets a free art check before production, so problems get caught early.
